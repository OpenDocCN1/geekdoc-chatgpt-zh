\( q(x_{t-1} \mid x_t, x_0) = \frac{q(x_{t-1})q(x_0 \mid x_{t-1})q(x_t \mid x_{t-1}, x_0)}{q(x_0)q(x_t \mid x_0)} = \frac{q(x_{t-1} \mid x_0)q(x_t \mid x_{t-1}, x_0)}{q(x_t \mid x_0)} \)

不妨将 \( q(x_{t-1} \mid x_t, x_0) \) 的均值和方差表示为 \( \widetilde{\mu}_t(x_t, x_0) \) 和 \( \widetilde{\beta}_t \)，即

\( q(x_{t-1} \mid x_t, x_0) = \mathcal{N}(x_{t-1}; \widetilde{\mu}_t(x_t, x_0), \widetilde{\beta}_t \mathbf{I}) \)

前面提到前向过程为马尔可夫链，并且任一时间步的 \( x_t \) 都可以使用 \( x_0 \) 和 \( \beta \) 来表示，那么可以将公式 (6-44) 中的每一分项分别表示为

\( q(x_t \mid x_{t-1}, x_0) = q(x_t \mid x_{t-1}) = \mathcal{N}(x_t; \sqrt{1-\beta_t}x_{t-1}, \beta_t \mathbf{I}) \)

\( q(x_{t-1} \mid x_0) = \mathcal{N}(x_{t-1}; \sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}}x_0, (1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}) \mathbf{I}) \)

\( q(x_t \mid x_0) = \mathcal{N}(x_t; \sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}x_0, (1-\bar{\alpha}_t) \mathbf{I}) \)

对于一个一元高斯分布 \( x \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2) \)，其概率密度函数为 \( f(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma}\exp\left(-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}\right) \)。参照这一定义，将上述每一分项高斯分布的概率密度函数的指数部分代入，有

\( q(x_{t-1} \mid x_t, x_0) = \frac{q(x_{t-1} \mid x_0)q(x_t \mid x_{t-1}, x_0)}{q(x_t \mid x_0)} \propto \frac{\exp\left(-\frac{(x_{t-1} - \sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}}x_0)^2}{2(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1})}\right)\exp\left(-\frac{(x_t - \sqrt{1-\beta_t}x_{t-1})^2}{2\beta_t}\right)}{\exp\left(-\frac{(x_t - \sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}x_0)^2}{2(1-\bar{\alpha}_t)}\right)} = \exp\left(-\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{(x_{t-1} - \sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}}x_0)^2}{(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1})} + \frac{(x_t - \sqrt{1-\beta_t}x_{t-1})^2}{\beta_t} - \frac{(x_t - \sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}x_0)^2}{(1-\bar{\alpha}_t)}\right)\right) = \exp\left(-\frac{1}{2}\left(\underbrace{\left(\frac{1}{1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}} + \frac{1-\beta_t}{\beta_t}\right)}_{1} x_{t-1}^2 - \underbrace{\left(\frac{2\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}}x_0}{1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}} + \frac{2\sqrt{1-\beta_t}x_t}{\beta_t}\right)}_{2} x_{t-1} + C(x_t, x_0)\right)\right) \)

其中，\( C(x_t, x_0) \) 为与 \( x_{t-1} \) 无关项的组合，可以忽略。此外，一元高斯分布概率密度函数的指数部分可以展开： \( \exp\left(-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}\right) = \exp\left(-\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{\sigma^2}x^2 - \frac{2\mu}{\sigma^2}x + \frac{\mu^2}{\sigma^2}\right)\right) \)，\( \frac{1}{\sigma^2} \) 和 \( \frac{2\mu}{\sigma^2} \) 分别对应公式 (6-49) 中的 ① 部分和 ② 部分，即

\( \frac{1}{\tilde{\beta}_t} = \underbrace{\left(\frac{1}{1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}} + \frac{1-\beta_t}{\beta_t}\right)}_{0} = \frac{1-\bar{\alpha}_t}{(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1})\beta_t} \)
