\( E_i(y_i, \hat{y}_i) = -y_i \log \hat{y}_i, \quad (3-5) \n E(y, \hat{y}) = \sum_i E_i(y_i, \hat{y}_i) = - \sum_i y_i \log \hat{y}_i, \quad (3-6) \)

根据反向传播算法，需要求 E 对 U、V、W 的梯度：

\( \frac{\partial E}{\partial V} = \sum_i \frac{\partial E_i}{\partial V}, \quad (3-7) \n \frac{\partial E}{\partial W} = \sum_i \frac{\partial E_i}{\partial W}, \quad (3-8) \n \frac{\partial E}{\partial U} = \sum_i \frac{\partial E_i}{\partial U}, \quad (3-9) \)

如图 3-4 所示，求 E 对 V 的梯度，需要分别求各个时刻的误差 E_i 对 V 的梯度再求和，以求 E_3 对 V 的梯度为例：

\( \frac{\partial E_3}{\partial V} = \frac{\partial E_3}{\partial \hat{y}_3} \frac{\partial \hat{y}_3}{\partial V} = \frac{\partial E_3}{\partial \hat{y}_3} \frac{\partial \hat{y}_3}{\partial z_3} \frac{\partial z_3}{\partial V}, \quad (3-10) \)

其中，\( z_3 = V h_3 \)，同样求得其他时刻误差对 V 的梯度，求和可得 \( \frac{\partial E}{\partial V} \circ \)。
