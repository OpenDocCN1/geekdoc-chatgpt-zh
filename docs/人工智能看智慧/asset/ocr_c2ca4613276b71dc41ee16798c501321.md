# The Relationship between Science Fiction and Science Popularization: A discussion based on historical and conceptual analysis

LIU Bing$^{1,2}$ FENG Xi-ge$^{3}$

(Institute for Cultural Heritage and History of Science & Technology, University of Science and Technology Beijing; $^2$Center for Science Communication and Popularization of CAST and Tsinghua University; $^3$Department of the History of Science, School of Humanities, Tsinghua University)

Abstract: For a long time, the relationship between science fiction and science popularization has been a controversial issue. Based on a brief historical review of the development of science fiction and science popularization in China, this paper combined historical discussion with conceptual analysis, and demonstrated that the "science and literature controversy" between science fiction and science popularization can be deconstructed to some extent, which means that science fiction can be accommodated in science popularization under the concept of "big science popularization".

Keywords: science fiction, science popularization, relationship, science-literature controversy
