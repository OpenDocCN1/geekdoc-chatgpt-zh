\(\hat{y}(v, x) := \sum_{i=1}^{n-1} \sum_{j=i+1}^n \langle v_{i_{sf_j}}, v_{j_{sf_i}} \rangle x_i x_j\) (5-22)

损失函数包含参数的正则项和log损失：

\(L = L_{reg} + L_{err} = \frac{\lambda}{2} \| v \|_2^2 + \sum_{i=1}^m \log(1 + \exp(-y \cdot \hat{y}(v, x)))\) (5-23)

参数的梯度如下：

\(g_{i_{sf_j}} = \lambda \cdot v_{i_{sf_j}} + \frac{\partial L_{err}}{\partial \hat{y}} \cdot v_{j_{sf_i}} \cdot x_i x_j\) (5-24)

\(\frac{\partial L_{err}}{\partial \hat{y}} = \frac{-y}{1 + \exp(\hat{y})}\)

\((G_{i_{sf_j}})_d = (G_{i_{sf_j}})_d + (g_{i_{sf_j}})_d^2\) (5-25)

\((v_{i_{sf_j}})_d = (v_{i_{sf_j}})_d - \frac{\eta}{\sqrt{(G_{i_{sf_j}})_d}} (g_{i_{sf_j}})_d\) (5-26)
