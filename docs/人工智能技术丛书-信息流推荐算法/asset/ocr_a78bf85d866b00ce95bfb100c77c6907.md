\(\nabla_\theta J^C(u) = \nabla_\theta \mathbb{E}_{(\widetilde{t_p}, \widetilde{t_n}) \sim p_\theta(u)} [\log(1 - D(\widetilde{i_p}, \widetilde{i_n}))]\)
\(=\)
\(\sum_{(\widetilde{t_p}, \widetilde{t_n}) \in t_p \notin t_N} \nabla_\theta p_\theta(u) \log(1 + \exp(f_\theta(\widetilde{i_p}, \widetilde{i_n})))\)
\(=\)
\(\sum_{(\widetilde{t_p}, \widetilde{t_n}) \in t_p \notin t_N} p_\theta(u) \nabla_\theta \log(p_\theta(u)) \log(1 + \exp(f_\theta(\widetilde{i_p}, \widetilde{i_n}))) \quad (9-55)\)
\(=\)
\(\mathbb{E}_{(\widetilde{t_p}, \widetilde{t_n}) \sim p_\theta(u)} [\nabla_\theta \log(p_\theta(u)) \log(1 + \exp(f_\theta(\widetilde{i_p}, \widetilde{i_n})))]\)
\(\approx\)
\(\frac{1}{K} \sum_{k=1}^K \nabla_\theta \log(p_\theta(u)) \log(1 + \exp(f_\theta(\widetilde{i_p}, \widetilde{i_n})))\)

整体的优化目标如下：

\(\mathcal{O} = \min_\theta \max_\omega \mathcal{O}_G + \lambda \sum_{u \in d} \mathbb{E}_{(t_p \cup t_n) \sim p_{nmc}(t_p \cup t_n)} [\sigma(f_\theta(i_p, i_n))] + \sum_{u \in d} \mathbb{E}_{(\widetilde{t_p}, \widetilde{t_n}) \sim p_\theta(u)} [\log(1 - D(\widetilde{i_p}, \widetilde{i_n}))]\)
\((9-56)\)

实际训练中，生成网络G可以先根据自编码器的损失预训练好。判决网络也可以根据真实的热门、长尾物品对来预训练。
