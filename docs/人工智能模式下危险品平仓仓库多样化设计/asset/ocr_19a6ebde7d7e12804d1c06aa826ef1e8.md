\( P_{case2} = \left[ 1 - \left( r_p \right)^{10} - \left( r_q \right)^{10} \right]^2 \)

\( P_{case3} = \left[ 1 - \left( r_p \right)^{9} - \left( r_q \right)^{9} \right]^2 \)
