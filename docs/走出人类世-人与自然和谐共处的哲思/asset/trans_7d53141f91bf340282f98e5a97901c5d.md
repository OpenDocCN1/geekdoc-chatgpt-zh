

# Reflections on Pandemic, Civilization, and High-Tech Worship

## Pandemic and the Absence of the "Third Pole": Reconstructing Civilizational Order from a "Life-Based" Perspective

In the history of humans as a biological species, various pandemics have always occurred, but they also always pass. Every pandemic, when it strikes, brings shock to the society of that time. However, some pandemics, after passing, leave the original society disintegrated, such as the European plague in the 16th century that led to the demise of the Aztec Empire; some pandemics, after passing, bring earth-shattering changes to society, such as the Black Death in 14th-century Europe that triggered the Renaissance; and still others, after passing, see society continue to operate in its inherent mode. Judging by the technological capabilities humanity currently possesses, the current COVID-19 pandemic will eventually pass.

But will the contradictions and conflicts mentioned above—those that exist due to the absence of the "Third Pole" and cannot be resolved within the existing "anthropocentric" or "theocentric" frameworks—disappear with the passing of the pandemic? Absolutely not. So what should we do? For those who care about the common destiny of humanity, instead of falling into needless anxiety over the pandemic, it is better to think seriously about and discuss where to find the "ultimate basis for the standards of right and wrong that govern behavior" that can transcend the "anthropocentric" or "theocentric" frameworks and serve globalized human society, or, in other words, where to reconstruct the "Third Pole" of a three-component system with a virtuous cycle that defines "order" and constrains "power." In my view, since humans are organisms, and the source of cognition is also the special attribute that arises from humans as living systems, human survival and development ultimately must obey the basic laws of living systems.

We know that cellular living systems, including the emergence of eukaryotic cells, have a history of 10^9 years (1 billion years), while human civilization has a history only on the order of 10^3 years (millennia). The Earth's biosphere had been operating for two to three billion years before humans appeared. The basic laws that maintain the continuity of living systems and made the emergence of humans possible should provide insights that cannot be ignored for humanity's search for the "Third Pole." A living system is not a specific material molecule, nor is it an abstract concept; it is a special mode of material existence. This special mode of material existence of living systems relies on the interaction of intermolecular forces; it is a relatively stable intermediate state, identifiable by humans, within a dynamic process of constant separation and combination. This system of thought, which takes the basic laws of living systems as the "ultimate basis for the standards of right and wrong that govern human behavior," can be called "Biocentrism" (or "Life-Based Philosophy"). How "Biocentrism" should be specifically articulated at the level of behavioral norms and standards of right and wrong exceeds the scope allowed by the length of this article. But it provides a completely new approach for us to define the "order" of human society and constrain the "power" that maintains order. Perhaps this will not only help resolve current contradictions and conflicts but also help humanity, as a responsible member of the Earth's biosphere, create a brand-new, sustainably developing civilization.

## Reflecting on High-Tech Worship from the COVID-19 Pandemic

**Zhang Xianglong**

![](images/00013.jpeg) ![p076](images/00001.jpeg)

Since the outbreak of the COVID-19 pandemic, the author, like hundreds of millions of compatriots, has been anxious day and night, longing for the arrival of the "turning point" and praying for the pandemic to subside. In 2003, we experienced "SARS," which shook the whole country. But this pandemic appears even more ferocious; the virus is more intractable and more infectious. At this moment, besides taking preventive measures within one's capacity, the author, secluded at home, cannot help but reflect on the warnings given to us by the repeated occurrence of such plagues. As of the time of writing this article[![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note20n), the author has seen many related commentaries with various leanings, but few, it seems, have linked this plague phenomenon to high-tech worship.

First, we must acknowledge two facts: First, pestilence is a disaster that frequently appears in human history; Second, Western-originated high technology, especially high technology in the fields of medicine and pharmacy, has been a sharp weapon in modern times for resisting certain diseases that threaten humans, such as penicillin for inflammatory infections, various vaccines for infectious diseases, surgery for cataracts, appendicitis, dental diseases, trauma, dystocia, and so on. Generally speaking, the high technology of modern medicine and its related cognition (such as cognition regarding the sources of diseases and the necessity of public health) have powerfully promoted the extension of human lifespan[![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note21n), which is undeniably a major achievement of humanity. However, there is another side to the matter. If these high technologies are revered as the sole truth in their relevant fields, excluding other technologies[![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note22n) and knowledge, one will fall into high-tech worship, and such worship is unfavorable, even dangerous, to the survival of the Chinese nation and even humanity. This article intends to argue this judgment in light of the current COVID-19 pandemic.

Based on the above, readers should already understand that the author does not oppose high technology itself, and gives high praise to the positive achievements of this high technology. The author believes that what needs to be criticized and discarded is merely the *worship* of high technology. Next, the author will mainly answer the following questions: What is "high-tech worship"? Why can high technology achieve such outstanding results? What are the limitations of high technology? What dangers has high-tech worship brought or may potentially bring? How are these limitations and worship manifested in this COVID-19 pandemic? How can the state of high-tech monopoly be transformed into the widespread application of appropriate technology?

### What is High-Tech Worship?

First, what is high technology? Literally, high technology is high-end, advanced, cutting-edge science and technology. But in terms of its substantive content, high technology is science and technology that has been fully objectified, can produce novel effects relatively quickly—new productive forces, commercial profits, Nobel Prizes, enhance the reputation of the "tech wizards," thereby enhancing the holder's control and influence over nature and others. The more precisely a technological product is objectified, that is, quantified and systematized in form, the more easily it becomes a force and means of transforming natural things and interpersonal relationships. The difference between Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) and Western medicine can illustrate this problem well. To this day, Western medicine's view of the human body and disease has become quite objectified. Western medicine understands the human body mainly through anatomy, thereby gaining knowledge of the operation of human tissues, organs, body fluids, and neural networks; later it discovered finer objects such as cells, bacteria, viruses, and genes, and uses them to explain the formation of the human body and the causes and treatments of diseases. In particular, the discovery of genes is regarded as humanity having obtained the "key" to understanding and transforming living bodies (including the human body). In contrast, and even opposite in some respects, TCM, although it has its own effective research methods, based on theories and hypotheses such as Yin-Yang Five Elements and Five Movements Six Qi, is full of temporal or opportunistic understanding, but its cognition of the human body is not precisely objectified or physically spatially localized. TCM's cognition of the "Five Viscera and Six Bowels" is not equivalent to the viscera in the anatomical sense (this once became one of the reasons TCM was attacked as unscientific), but is more of a functional, overlapping, or holistic cognition. Meridians are basically non-objectified, yet they are a real human bodily existence; reducing them to some physical object, such as the "cell-gap-connective tissue-blood vessel-lymph-body fluid (endocrine-immunity)-neural pluralistic system," even if helpful, cannot replace the intuitively self-evident understanding of meridians in TCM's original paradigm. TCM knows the physical condition and illness through pulse diagnosis, which cannot be reasonably explained by objectified causal relationships either. As for the Five Movements Six Qi and other temporal doctrines, they are even further removed from objectified thinking. Therefore, TCM is not high technology.

For another example, ancient Chinese invented black powder composed of sulfur, saltpeter, and charcoal. This seems to be the result of objectified research, but the actual situation is not entirely so; it is very different from the Western invention and application of gunpowder. The ancient Chinese used sulfur and saltpeter to treat diseases very early (hence called "medicine"); later, Taoists, in pursuit of longevity, used these medicines for alchemy, and in this process randomly discovered black powder. Afterward, black powder in China was used to make smoke and fire effects for acrobatics and circuses, or firecrackers and fireworks for exorcism and celebrations. Only later did black powder begin to be used for military purposes, but due to the lack of fully objectified research, its power was small, and it was difficult to be precisely applied to rapid firing and intense explosions of weapons. This situation did not attract strong attention from the court and the public, so this gunpowder in China also failed to dominate subsequent military development. Ancient China had many inventions, all disappearing inadvertently as objects like this; this was caused by the thinking patterns and values of the ancient Chinese, who had an instinctive and rational vigilance and neglect toward fully objectified technology, whether Confucian or Taoist. Black powder was transmitted to the West via Arabs, influencing Westerners' empirical feelings. But Westerners, in a more precisely objectified way, created modern gunpowder. It originated in 1771 with picric acid synthesized by P. Wolfe of England, a yellow crystal, hence the name "yellow gunpowder." Over more than 100 years, gunpowder was improved many times by Nobel and others, becoming increasingly precisely objectified, and in the interconnected system of modern technology, evolved into modern gunpowder usable in machine guns, automatic rifles, explosive shells, and rockets, capable of killing like hemp and blasting holes through mountains.

It must be admitted that, so far, high technology originates from the West, that is, Europe and North America. The method of high technology is to objectify the object of study precisely and systematically, and to discover new breakthroughs through the combination of pre-constructed hypotheses and experimental testing. Therefore, its ideal research paradigm is the mathematics pioneered by ancient Greece, such as *Elements*, which possesses both formal precision and calculable deducibility and verifiability. Of course, to extend its tentacles into the material natural world, it needs repeated experiments, but these empirical practices serve the mathematized and theoretical-modelized research framework, thereby distinguishing it from the "empirical" technologies produced in other civilizations (this judgment is not entirely accurate, and will be discussed below).

Second, what is high-tech worship? It is a tendency of thought and ideology that regards high technology as the sole truth in every field and matter, to be unconditionally promoted worldwide, while viewing other research or practice methods in the same field and matter as heresies, at least as non-truths, which must be excluded and overthrown for peace of mind. The historical source or source of this mode of thinking of such worship mainly lies in the Western religious worship of the one supreme God. This religion firmly believes it is the sole truth about this world and life, and believers have the responsibility to spread it worldwide; at the same time, it vigorously excludes other religions, including internal opponents, regarding them as heresies instigated by the devil. When Western modern science, such as Copernican astronomy, appeared, it was once severely persecuted by the Catholic Church, and Bruno was even burned at the stake for it; but when this science and technology attained the ruling position of the Catholic Church in those days, certain people, especially some philosophers and even politicians, played the roles of church priests or inquisitors, proclaiming the monopoly of high technology on truth, and the inferiority, falsity (pseudoscience), and harmfulness of those alternative technologies different from it. In the words of the *Old Testament*, they defend "a jealous God" (*Old Testament, Exodus*, 20:5), who will absolutely not tolerate believers simultaneously believing in other gods or substitutes. In short, although the specific propositions of these high-tech worshippers differ from or even oppose those of the Catholic Church, their mode of thinking is consistent: both are self-exclusive, binary, and fight to the death. Some historians of science have also argued with evidence that there is some intrinsic relationship between the emergence of Western modern science and Western religion.[![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note23n)

Some may say that the essence of science and religion is different; science can be empirically verified, thereby benefiting humanity, but religion cannot. Religion might retort: scientific discoveries are only (temporarily) verified as effective in certain fields, but in those matters concerning the great issues of life, such as the acquisition of life's meaning, the value basis for making major decisions, the reconstruction of confidence after repeated setbacks, scientific doctrines are ineffective, while the truth of religion has been verified by countless life experiences.

The history of science shows that the scientific truth of one era may be corrected by new discoveries of a later era, or even overturned in certain key aspects, such as Newtonian mechanics being corrected by relativity, the setting of flat space being overturned. Certain discoveries of quantum mechanics even subverted multiple previous scientific truths, which even Einstein could not accept at the time. This shows that the so-called truth grasped through high technology is not truth in the absolute sense (if that means complete conformity with reality and thus bringing happiness to life), but merely some knowledge and techniques that enhance the power of the holder of high technology. Therefore, what high-tech worship truly worships is not truth, but power, and moreover ever-increasing power, because high technology is actually technology that is never high enough and must always be higher.

### Why Can High Technology Achieve Such Outstanding Results?

According to currently prevailing standards of measurement, relative to the technologies of other civilizations and cultures, high technology has achieved more remarkable achievements. What is the reason? As mentioned earlier, high technology operates through the combination of scientific hypotheses and experimental testing, and the spirit of Western formalized mathematics permeates the scientific hypotheses. The so-called experimental testing in experience, and even scientific hypotheses in a certain sense, are implemented and proposed by all kinds of technologies; only the "Western mathematical factor" in the hypotheses is unique to high technology. The "mathematical factor"[![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note24n) refers to the structure of a formalized game that allows one to play with oneself (there are always new combinatorial possibilities, and wins and losses can be adjudicated). Therefore, it possesses an inferential capability inherent in the form, allowing scientific hypotheses to extend preset tentacles beyond experience, such as conjecturing and calculating that there might be a planet in a certain place based on a certain gravitational anomaly, or deducing that light passing through a strong gravitational field will produce a certain angle of deflection based on certain theoretical presets. In this way, even if the results of later experiments are mostly not affirmative, overall, once the experiments guided by such hypotheses fit, the novelty and the depth of random triggering they achieve are unmatched by other types of experiments and empirical attempts. This mathematical factor profoundly influences the entire process of high-tech exploration; even in certain disciplines, such as biology and medicine in the 18th and 19th centuries, which could not yet fully embody mathematical form at the time. This is the pattern Kant spoke of: "man legislates for nature." Through the supposedly a priori valid mathematical forms and conceptualized causal reasoning, humans form scientific theories and experimental designs that can question nature with precise objectification; in repeated testing, theoretical correction, and even revolution, they rigorously cross-examine nature, forcing it to divulge secrets that would otherwise remain hidden in other circumstances. Therefore, scientific exploration and technological development in this research paradigm are not merely observation, induction, digestion, association, extraction, and even slow comprehension of experience (as in generalized natural history), but active and cruel (think of those experiments done on living animals) positioning and interrogation. Consequently, the results it obtains—whether positive or negative—are more prominent, fully objectified, endless (there is absolutely no saying of "resting in the highest good"), and astonishing (if not terrifying).

### The Limitations of High Technology and the Sleight of Hand of High-Tech Worship

The above discussion lets us see that the terrifying innovative power of high technology is built upon its obsession. The mathematical factor is both ingenious and obsessive; it lets people see only the existence and value of that which stands out in form, and tends toward an `A∨￢A` (either A or not-A) binary selection architecture. Interrogating nature with scientific hypotheses plus radicalized experiments, and actually managing to extract answers, is of course a remarkable achievement, yet it violates common sense; the key here is still the mathematical factor. Mathematics, this seemingly narcissistic—intuitively self-evident purely formalized chess game—can reveal some key information about the vast universe and the myriad world; for the vision of daily experience, it is truly an inconceivable matter. The ancient Greek Pythagoras proposed "number is the origin of all things," but did not state a very pertinent reason. Kant gave a reason for this in the *Critique of Pure Reason*[![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note25n): mathematics is the expression of the pure forms of human sensible intuition (space and time), and humans can only cope with the world through this form, just like you are forever wearing a pair of tinted glasses. If there is a kind of knowledge, namely mathematics, that can display the internal structural mechanism of these glasses (first and foremost the lenses), then it will certainly be of great help for you to understand the secrets of the world, at least able to give important hints. Furthermore, this pure form of intuition, by combining the transcendental imagination[![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note26n) with concepts of the understanding, is more conducive to forming a priori anticipations or presentiments of the world. Unfortunately, Kant later felt that the central position of this transcendental imagination posed a threat to the formation of the doctrine of conceptualized reason or apperception, so he greatly lowered its status, to the point of blocking people's in-depth discussion of it.[![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note27n)
