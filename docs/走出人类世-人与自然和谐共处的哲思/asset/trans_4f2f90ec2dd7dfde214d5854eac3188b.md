

# Kant's Oversight and Reflections on High-Tech Worship

## Kant's Oversight and the Non-Monolithic Nature of the "A Priori" Paradigm

It is evident that although Kant's answer is highly enlightening, it also contains a major oversight. To be more specific, it is dogmatic to assert that Western mathematics is the sole expression of the pure forms of sensible intuition, and that Newtonian physics is the sole expression of physical causality. For instance, his taking Euclidean geometry as the unique manifestation of the pure form of space was self-evident to him, just as the statement "through a point outside a line, only one parallel line can be drawn" was self-evident at that time. However, the subsequent discovery of non-Euclidean geometry demonstrated that there can be more than one geometry; through a point outside a line, more than one parallel line may be drawn. Although this oversight seems to lie only within Western mathematics and science, this counterexample at the foundation (combined with Kuhn's theory of "paradigms") still shows that the so-called "a priori" valid paradigms, or the "necessary prejudices" that human cognition of the world inevitably carries, are not monolithic, nor must they be formalized mathematical ones. As long as a "research paradigm" [![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note28n) can be formed, possessing a certain derivational force of a certain morphology, it has the ability to extend theoretical presuppositions and thereby guide experiments. For example, mathematics need not be so formalized, as demonstrated by China's *The Nine Chapters on the Mathematical Art*; even the numerical and symbolic system of the *Book of Changes (Yi Jing)* is an architecture with a certain a priori derivational force. Moreover, a "paradigm" includes but differs from specific research methods; its normative form is self-referential and all-encompassing—that is, it itself contains the norms for the paradigm, and there is no single standard capable of measuring the merits of all paradigms. It is thus clear that different research paradigms have different strengths and weaknesses. The strengths of the high-tech research paradigm lie in mathematical formalization, precise objectification, empowerment, and competitiveness; the standards that advocate these strengths (such as "higher, faster, stronger") also belong to this paradigm. Therefore, other paradigms in the past—Confucian, Daoist, Buddhist, pantheistic, even Aristotelian—did not agree with such standards.

From this we can know that the "a priori" [![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note29n) does not equal the "unique." Humans are not gods and possess no completely transparent cognitive mode that transcends their mode of existence—physiological structure, language used, environment inhabited, culture borne, challenges faced. There are always a priori premises or a priori colorations caused by instinct and circumstance. Scientists are also people living in their situations or "forms of life" (Wittgenstein); therefore, any scientific research must have its a priori presuppositions or research paradigms. However, as analyzed above, the pure forms or pure symbols of space and time that constitute these a priori presuppositions are non-objectifiable; therefore, they would never be so pure as to have only one form of expression. The "pure image" (reines Bild) or "schema" constructed by the "transcendental imagination" [![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note30n) exceeds objectifiable forms (including the objectification of concepts), just as the pure triangle exceeds any concrete triangle (but is not yet a concept), and also exceeds the abstract triangle prescribed by Euclidean geometry or any geometric paradigm. We may even ask: must a triangle be only two-dimensional or three-dimensional, and not four-dimensional or five-dimensional? "Reproductive imagination" cannot reach beyond three dimensions, but "transcendental, productive imagination" possibly can.

## The Danger and Asymmetry of High-Tech Worship

High-tech worship is completely blind to the diversity of technological research paradigms; it touts high technology as the sole truth within technology, suppresses other technologies, and ideologically rigidifies the inherent—even from a certain angle reasonable—bias carried by high technology, thereby blocking the reasonable possibilities of studying nature from other angles.

High-tech achievements are sometimes dangerous to humanity, even extremely dangerous. Examples include certain chemical products (plastics, pesticides, poison gases, etc.), exhaust emissions, thermonuclear bombs, various pollutants and waste (including nuclear waste), nanomaterials, genetic modification, and super artificial intelligence (yet to be realized). This fact is currently almost common knowledge among intellectuals with reflective capacity. Other technological paradigms, such as ancient Chinese technology, ancient Egyptian technology, or Indigenous American technologies, although they could generally meet the survival needs of their times, did not bring such dangers. Furthermore, driven by the "must go higher" nature of high technology, its future products will possess even greater power to transform the basic structure of the world, and thus will be even more dangerous. But high-tech worship prevents people from seeing the urgency and lethality of this danger clearly, or makes people believe that even if such danger exists, it can only be solved by even higher technology, with absolutely no possibility of solving the problem by replacing or partially replacing it with other technologies. This is tantamount to saying that a group of bureaucrats committed corruption and abuse of power, but only they themselves or their own institutions can correct it. In short, high-tech worship suppresses people's transcendental imagination, confining it within the images and value structures created by modernity and the high-tech paradigm. One might even say this is a new Inquisition that stifles the freedom of humanity's fundamental thought.

### High Technology and High-Tech Worship in the COVID-19 Pandemic

This COVID-19 pandemic has exposed some limitations of high technology, particularly the loopholes of high-tech worship. First, merely a dozen years after the appearance of SARS, a similar virus has once again triggered a large-scale epidemic. This shows that high technology still lacks effective preventive measures and treatments for new epidemic diseases threatening populations. Regarding the epidemic, high technology provided no clear early warning beforehand; even after the COVID-19 outbreak, besides administrative negligence and arbitrariness hampering prevention and control, high technology itself had problems. For instance, high-tech experts in some countries confidently declared in the early stages of the epidemic that it was "preventable and controllable," or claimed that "wearing masks is of little use." But the epidemic soon spun out of control, leading to the implementation of a series of exceptionally strong measures such as city lockdowns, area closures, flight suspensions, work stoppages, and school closures. Moreover, high technology currently cannot provide specific drugs that directly eliminate the virus or targeted vaccines. In the near future, vaccines or response drugs may be developed, but the optimal window for effective prevention and control of the epidemic has already been missed. In many cases, this is not due to the incompetence of technical personnel, but is determined by the characteristics of high technology. The research method of precise objectification requires the object to appear, and to be carefully identified (e.g., at the genetic level), before it can be studied. But the pathogens of infectious diseases are not fully objectifiable entities. You can use vaccines to control or even eradicate smallpox, and respond relatively effectively to cholera, malaria, and schistosomiasis. But pathogens are living things; some of them mutate over time, adapt to new drugs and human environments, and develop so-called drug resistance. So within the horizon of high technology, we still cannot see the possibility of eradicating all epidemics, nor even see effective methods for predicting and controlling major epidemics.

Regarding the origin of this pandemic, there is still no definitive conclusion. On the surface, it first broke out in a province in China and then appeared in many other countries. But some retrospective studies show that the virus causing the pandemic already existed in the world at an earlier time. This puzzling situation shows that high technology is not omnipotent; it is at a loss when dealing with life forms that are not so fully objectifiable. We often hear voices saying: "This is a problem for experts to solve." But the problem is that experts often disagree on such issues, to the extent that certain ill-intentioned politicians can exploit one of the views to manipulate the situation. Moreover, the rapid transport tools created by high technology and the resulting globalized flow have not only accelerated the speed of epidemic spread but also made tracing the virus source more difficult.

Second, there was once a rumor that the novel coronavirus causing this pandemic was artificially synthesized. As for who synthesized it and for what purpose, there were different versions. The author of this article once saw and heard some reports and audio-visual materials, presented by some reputable scientists and virus experts, seemingly proving this assertion with conclusive evidence. Later, 28 scientists signed a joint letter refuting this conclusion. Regardless of whether this rumor is true or false (I personally have always leaned towards the latter), it indicates a reality: current high technology has the capability, or at least a high probability, of manufacturing the novel coronavirus or even more terrifying viruses. When those experts argued for affirmation or negation, they were only debating whether the novel coronavirus *was* artificially synthesized, not whether existing high technology *could* manufacture it. This illustrates another problem: high technology has the capability to create things that can greatly harm humanity, yet lacks the capability to effectively and timely control them (see the discussion above). This is extremely dangerous! Moreover, this asymmetry appears to be widespread. Since the 20th century, humans have been able to manufacture atomic bombs, but have never found effective, sustainable, and feasible means to eliminate the ecological consequences of nuclear war or handle nuclear waste. Humans have been able to develop chemical fuels like petroleum and manufacture thousands of chemical products, such as astronomical numbers of plastic bags, yet have little technological capability to eliminate the greenhouse effect, offshore oil spills, and the long-term, irreversible pollution damage caused by chemical products to humans, land, water, and air. Furthermore, high technology will have the capability to alter human genes, yet does not know how to handle the relationship between such high-tech superhumans and normal humans, let alone how to make superhumans live happily. High technology may create powerful artificial intelligence, yet does not know how to ensure it only benefits humanity rather than harming it. Rules like the "Three Laws of Robotics" (from Asimov) may be self-contradictory in themselves; adding more auxiliary laws only makes the situation more complex and unpredictable. In a word, high technology has the capability to create both angels and demons, but lacks the capability to put the demons back into the bottle.

Third, due to the high-tech medical research angle of fully objectifying the human body, the vast majority of its treatment protocols target pathogens and affected organs, adopting direct confrontation—a "God versus Devil" strategy, that is, a "militarized" strategy of killing pathogens (or stimulating antibodies to kill them) and removing diseased parts. This strategy is necessary and very effective in some cases, but is destined to have major defects, because the fully objectified response mode cannot keep pace with the flow of life time. For instance, the mutation of pathogens and the development of drug resistance mentioned above are immeasurable and untreatable by high-tech medicine. Moreover, this treatment mode produces two adverse consequences—increasing difficulty in identifying diseases, especially epidemic infectious diseases, and the toxic side effects of drugs and surgery. New pathogens and their variants will always appear, so distinguishing which disease the current epidemic phenomena belong to will become increasingly tedious, specialized, and difficult, requiring long-term trained professionals and high-end equipment. Especially during epidemics, such as winter and spring for influenza, SARS, and COVID-19, it is considerably difficult to reliably distinguish them among the signs of the swarming patients, promptly detect new or highly threatening ones, so that administrative departments can take decisive measures. When the COVID-19 pandemic first appeared (e.g., in the US), it was once regarded as flu and ignored; later, some sensitive individuals discovered anomalies and "blew the whistle" to warn, but were ignored because they were hastily viewed as "SARS." Even when doctors like Dr. Zhang Jixian identified it as a new epidemic and reported it (December 27, 2019), because they could not immediately determine the exact (fully objectified) information about the disease, they failed to break through the various noises of the epidemic situation, and thus could not decisively influence professional authorities and administrative decisions, leading to the explosive expansion of the epidemic within three weeks. Dr. Zhang Wenhong discussed this dilemma in a recent article [![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note31n), but his proposed improvements only call for establishing departments of infectious diseases and clinical microbiology according to international standards, making our already powerful CDC direct reporting system even stronger, "blocking the enemy at the first line, rather than waiting for the CDC to clean up after a city-wide outbreak." [![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note32n) If the state has strong financial resources, establishing these departments and strengthening relevant personnel training can indeed enhance early warning capabilities; combined with strict control of epidemic outbreak points, it is indeed an effective temporary response measure. But can this fundamentally solve the chronic ills of objectified research and treatment discussed above? Basically impossible, or at least doubtful.

As for the other problem of high-tech medicine—the toxic side effects of drugs and surgery—it is also alarming. For example, cancer surgery and postoperative chemotherapy destroy the body's immunity. Some diseases require patients to take medication for life, which easily leads to the accumulation of side effects into new ailments. Although almost all drugs, including Chinese medicine, have side effects, because high-tech drugs mostly target specific pathogenic objects, they find it even harder to consider the needs of the whole body, so the side effects should be greater and stronger. For the human body, a spiritual living organism, there is no "profit without loss" business. Chinese medicine emphasizes attention to the overall vitality of the human body and mind (the primordial qi generated by the interaction of Yin and Yang); diagnosis and medication are strongly time-dependent and weakly object-dependent; prescriptions emphasize the complementary matching effects of "Monarch, Minister, Assistant, and Courier" (Jun-Chen-Zuo-Shi) among drugs; and they value "treating the undiseased"—treating or preventing diseases that have not yet been objectified. Therefore, overall, the toxic side effects should be much smaller. If the application of patent Chinese medicines is as rigid as Western drugs, some indeed produce non-negligible toxic side effects, but in the original paradigm of Chinese medicine, these can be avoided or weakened.

High-tech worship in this COVID-19 pandemic has also manifested very clearly. First, the prevention and control system is basically, even completely, high-tech-based. For instance, the situation reflected in Dr. Zhang Wenhong's article cited above—although this system has repeatedly erred, people still insist on the monopoly of the high-tech system, refusing to let other technologies intervene in key issues or judgments. A video circulated online showing that at a meeting organized by the China Academy of Chinese Medical Sciences on June 27, 2019, TCM doctor and scholar Academician Wang Yongyan predicted in a formal speech: "In the second half of the year, especially around the Winter Solstice, continuing into the spring of next year, there will be a warm epidemic (Wen Yi) outbreak." [![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note33n) The Winter Solstice in 2019 was December 22. According to existing information, the first case of the Wuhan epidemic appeared in early December 2019 (just before the Winter Solstice), and the formal outbreak occurred in January 2020 (just after the Winter Solstice). In retrospect, this prediction made half a year before the event was at least basically accurate regarding the timing of the epidemic outbreak. Regarding the duration of the epidemic ("into the spring of next year"), it also appears roughly correct. If the epidemic in China had continued into summer or even autumn, this prediction would have been flawed [![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note34n).

In response, the *New Beijing Daily* and others immediately voiced serious skepticism [![](images/00007.png)](#part00005.html#note35n), calling it a "divine prediction" or "mystical prediction," completely different from "scientific prediction." An article in the *New Beijing Daily* proposed standards for distinguishing the two: "This kind of scientific prediction is based on clearly grasped laws, through systematic collation, summary, and further research to give a prophecy. Scientists write it into papers, organize the most basic data and knowledge into a system, display it to peers according to logical processes, and repeatedly explain and discuss the difficult and key points. Moreover, all these can be questioned and tested." [![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note36n) But these standards cannot completely negate Academician Wang Yongyan's prediction, because, as another online article [![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note37n) states, Academician Wang's prediction was also based on clearly grasped, data-supported laws; it's just that they are not high-tech laws, but the laws of the operation of Yin-Yang, Five Elements, and Five Movements and Six Qi (Wu Yun Liu Qi) in Chinese medicine, which have their evidence and knowledge, and can also be questioned and tested (the above statement already implies this). As for repeated explanation, discussion, and writing papers, the Chinese medicine community certainly can and should do so. It is a pity that Academician Wang's prediction seems not to have been accompanied by detailed relevant research and argumentation, which is one reason it was ignored. However, even so, it does not mean that the Chinese medicine research paradigm cannot make scientific predictions; it only shows the current lack of Chinese medicine research strength. If Academician Wang's prediction had received the attention it deserved, it would not only have received serious study and testing within the Chinese medicine community, but also among cross-paradigm scholars.

That article in the *New Beijing Daily* represents the basic characteristics of the currently prevalent high-tech worship: monopolizing the right to interpret science in the name of science, thereby corrupting the nature of science which must ultimately be open to empirical facts; for "others" or non-high-tech technologies that are heterogeneous to it, it uniformly confuses them with the highly lethal labels of "mysticism" or even "primitive myths, religious stories to folk legends." It rejected Academician Wang's prediction as follows: "On the contrary, mystical prediction will not clearly tell you what the law is, nor will it break down the basic principles for you to see. Only after the fact do you try to explain by leaning towards what has already happened. So mystical prophecy has no significance for the future." I have argued above that Academician Wang's prediction is of major significance for the future (which we are currently in), and if it had received the reasonable attention it deserved, it would have had even greater significance. But what is heartbreaking is that countless such observations and inventions that originally possessed or would have possessed significance have been excluded and strangled with such high-sounding and overbearing reasons. It is only because this epidemic has had a huge impact and high technology's response has not been very effective so far that Academician Wang's prediction entered the public view.

In addition, in light of the experience of responding to SARS previously, this time Chinese medicine intervened relatively early in the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of COVID-19, and to date has played a positive role that is different from but not to be ignored compared to high technology. [![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note38n) For example, according to a report, the "Chinese medicine treatment plan at Beijing Ditan Hospital was not only effective for mild and moderate COVID-19 patients, but also achieved certain efficacy for severe patients. Preliminary analysis showed that the effective rate of symptomatic treatment with Chinese medicine alone was 87.5%, and the effective rate of integrated Chinese and Western medicine was 92.3%." [![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note39n) Many other hospitals and regions reported similar or significant effects, such as Guangdong, Zhejiang, Sichuan, Shanxi, and Yunnan. The same was true in Hubei, Wuhan. Academician Zhang Boli, a TCM expert participating in the anti-epidemic effort in Wuhan, said in an interview on February 28, 2020: "Now not only are all Fangcang shelter hospitals using integrated Chinese and Western medicine treatment, but severe patients in Jinyintan Hospital, Wuhan Pulmonary Hospital, and Wuhan Union Hospital have also begun joint Chinese-Western medicine consultations, with quite a few patients using Chinese medicine. Currently in Wuhan, the usage rate of Chinese medicine has risen from 30% in early February to over 80%." [![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note40n) Another example: Academician Huang Luqi discovered through frontline clinical observation in Wuhan: "Through integrated Chinese and Western medicine, mild patients' discomfort symptoms such as chest tightness disappeared faster, the treatment cycle for severe patients was shortened, and the average hospital stay for integrated treatment was less than that for Western medicine treatment alone." [![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note41n) According to more recent reports (early June 2020), overall, "over 90% of confirmed COVID-19 cases used Chinese medicine. Clinical efficacy observation showed a total effective rate of Chinese medicine over 90%." [![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note42n)

Academician Zhang Boli explained the anti-epidemic mechanism of Chinese medicine as follows: "Modern pharmacological research shows that many Chinese medicines have antipyretic and anti-inflammatory effects, can improve patients' fever symptoms, control the spread of lung inflammation, promote inflammation absorption, and play a role through multiple aspects, pathways, and targets. In addition, while antiviral, the advantage of Chinese medicine intervention also lies in its ability to regulate human immune function and stimulate the body's own defense capabilities." [![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note43n) He is explaining the effects of Chinese medicine anti-epidemic efforts using high-tech terminology and even strategies are necessary.

In summary, Chinese medicine, heterogeneous to high technology, has shown unique and remarkable performance in the prediction, diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of this epidemic. In some aspects (such as prediction), it played a role that high technology could not; in other aspects, it also has irreplaceable unique functions. The reason Chinese medicine could achieve these results is inseparable from the non-objectified, Yin-Yang time-dependent dimensions contained in Chinese medicine theory and practice. For example, the "Five Movements and Six Qi Theory" [![](images/00007.png)](#part0005.html#note45n) on which Academician Wang based his prediction is a theory based on the Yin-Yang Five Elements view, containing a non-objectified dimension of life time. Another example: the acupuncture suggested by Liu Lihong is premised on the presupposition of the existence of meridians, which cannot be fully objectified (anatomized). As for Chinese medicine's pulse diagnosis, prescriptions, etc., they are also therapies full of time-dependence and the body's on-site sensibility. Of course, they all have objectified dimensions—that is, they are realized as specific objective matters such as symptom differentiation, drug application, and treatment effect assessment at appropriate times and occasions. So Chinese medicine does not stop at "treating the undiseased"; it also has strategies for already formed diseases and even severe cases (such as severe patients in this epidemic). If the Chinese medicine research paradigm and historical heritage can be fully respected, then under current conditions, organic, flexible, and mutually equal "integration of Chinese and Western medicine," plus the pluralistic integration of various medical books such as Tibetan medicine and Mongolian medicine, is indeed a better line for epidemic prevention and control than a single Western medicine or Chinese medicine response mode. It is thus clear that the claim of high-tech worship in the medical field—that only high-tech medicine has grasped the truth about the human body, and that Chinese medicine and other alternative medical arts are not science at all, or even harmful quackery—is erroneous. Persisting in them brings only danger and disaster, not prosperity and strength, let alone harmonious and long-lasting beautiful survival.
