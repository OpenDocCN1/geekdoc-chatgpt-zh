4. 其他指令
(1) Understand Cultural Customs: Research the traditions and customs of the target culture to ensure references and metaphors in the translation are appropriate.
说明：了解文化习俗：调研目标文化的传统与风俗，确保译文中引例与隐喻恰当。

(2) Adapt Regional Color: Translate region-specific elements to equivalent items within the target culture, such as holidays, currency, and measurement units
说明：调适地域色彩：把地域特定元素换成本地等价物，如假日、货币、度量单位。

(3) Consider Religious Influences: Be mindful of the impact of religion on language and avoid religious offenses in translation.
说明：兼顾宗教影响：注意宗教对语言的影响，避免在翻译中产生冒犯。

(4) Balance Literal and Figurative Translation: Opt for a more figurative translation where necessary, especially with idioms, proverbs, and sayings.
说明：平衡直译与意译：必要时采用意译，尤其是处理成语、谚语和俗语。

(5) Investigate Historical Context: Understand the historical events related to the text's content for accurate interpretation and translation of references.
说明：探究历史背景：了解文本相关历史事件，以准确解读并翻译文中指涉。

(6) Utilize Localization Tools: Use localization software and tools to assist with particular cultural nuances of units, dates, and formats.
